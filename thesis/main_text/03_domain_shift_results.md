# Domain Shift Results

The benchmark begins with a domain-shift result rather than a modeling result. Hybrid and paired units are strongly separable even before modeling the targets, and the gap becomes extreme once recording-aware context is added. In [domain_classifier_summary.csv](/Users/paulruiz/Documents/Predicting_Good_Units/thesis/tables/02_domain_shift_hybrid_vs_paired/domain_classifier_summary.csv), unit-only features already produce near-perfect separation, while the full-context setting reaches the strongest separability in the thesis package. The corresponding overview figure is [domain_separability.png](/Users/paulruiz/Documents/Predicting_Good_Units/thesis/figures/02_domain_shift_hybrid_vs_paired/domain_separability.png).

The PCA projections show that the paired domain is not a single compact target distribution. In [pca_2d_by_domain.png](/Users/paulruiz/Documents/Predicting_Good_Units/thesis/figures/02_domain_shift_hybrid_vs_paired/pca_2d_by_domain.png) and [pca_2d_paired_by_family.png](/Users/paulruiz/Documents/Predicting_Good_Units/thesis/figures/02_domain_shift_hybrid_vs_paired/pca_2d_paired_by_family.png), paired recordings occupy multiple regions, with family structure that is only partially aligned with the hybrid source cloud. This supports two later design choices: recording-disjoint evaluation as the primary benchmark, and leave-one-family-out evaluation as a robustness stress test.

The shifted-feature summaries further show that the mismatch is not limited to one feature block. [feature_shift_summary.csv](/Users/paulruiz/Documents/Predicting_Good_Units/thesis/tables/02_domain_shift_hybrid_vs_paired/feature_shift_summary.csv) and [top_feature_shift.png](/Users/paulruiz/Documents/Predicting_Good_Units/thesis/figures/02_domain_shift_hybrid_vs_paired/top_feature_shift.png) show broad movement across waveform, amplitude, and QC-instability features. This is why the thesis does not treat hybrid data as a drop-in surrogate for paired data. The source domain is useful, but it requires explicit correction.
