# fpos Results

The `fpos` chapter is the main positive result of the thesis because it shows a consistent progression from weak basic transfer to a strong final target-domain model. The starting point for simple hybrid-to-paired transfer is the raw-feature XGBoost baseline in [fpos_benchmark_ladder.csv](/Users/paulruiz/Documents/Predicting_Good_Units/thesis/tables/04_fpos_model_progression/fpos_benchmark_ladder.csv): `Hybrid+paired basic XGBoost` reaches `R² = 0.3585` with `MAE = 0.1387`. Pure source transfer is substantially worse, with negative `R²`, which confirms that source supervision alone is not enough.

The first important jump comes from adding recording-aware context and source-derived stacked signals. `Paired-only context` reaches `R² = 0.4695`, and `Context-first source stack` reaches `R² = 0.5005`. These results establish the central modeling pattern of the thesis: the strongest models are not end-to-end transfer networks, but context-aware tabular models that treat source predictions as priors to be corrected in the paired domain.

The second jump comes from using unlabeled paired structure conservatively. `Pre-waveform unlabeled structure` reaches `R² = 0.5331`, showing that trust-aware pseudo supervision and anchor-style structural features add real value when they operate on real paired-domain `x`. The final jump comes from adding a learned waveform representation on top of that stack. The thesis winner, `Waveform winner`, reaches `R² = 0.5479` with `MAE = 0.1140`. The benchmark ladders are visualized in [fpos_benchmark_r2.png](/Users/paulruiz/Documents/Predicting_Good_Units/thesis/figures/04_fpos_model_progression/fpos_benchmark_r2.png) and [fpos_benchmark_mae.png](/Users/paulruiz/Documents/Predicting_Good_Units/thesis/figures/04_fpos_model_progression/fpos_benchmark_mae.png).

The ablation tables show that the improvements are structured rather than accidental. In [fpos_ablation_source_supervision.csv](/Users/paulruiz/Documents/Predicting_Good_Units/thesis/tables/04_fpos_model_progression/fpos_ablation_source_supervision.csv), raw transfer improves over pure source but remains clearly below context-first models. In [fpos_ablation_unlabeled_paired_structure.csv](/Users/paulruiz/Documents/Predicting_Good_Units/thesis/tables/04_fpos_model_progression/fpos_ablation_unlabeled_paired_structure.csv), the pseudo and anchor stage adds most of the pre-waveform gain. In [fpos_ablation_waveform_representation.csv](/Users/paulruiz/Documents/Predicting_Good_Units/thesis/tables/04_fpos_model_progression/fpos_ablation_waveform_representation.csv), the final waveform representation delivers the last improvement.

The per-recording and per-family summaries are important because they keep the result honest. [fpos_family_wave_summary.csv](/Users/paulruiz/Documents/Predicting_Good_Units/thesis/tables/04_fpos_model_progression/fpos_family_wave_summary.csv) and [fpos_recording_wave_summary.csv](/Users/paulruiz/Documents/Predicting_Good_Units/thesis/tables/04_fpos_model_progression/fpos_recording_wave_summary.csv) show that the final model is not uniformly better everywhere, but it improves the broad benchmark enough to become the main thesis result under the recording-disjoint protocol.
